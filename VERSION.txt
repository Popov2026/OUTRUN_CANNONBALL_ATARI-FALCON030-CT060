Cannonball Atari Falcon 030 / CT60 / CT63 port - v0.30 beta

Built with GCC (m68k-atari-mint-g++ 4.6.4) - see build_atari.sh / build_release.sh.

Changes since v0.27:
  Sound
  - FM on the DSP (fm_dsp = 1, default): the YM2151 FM chip is computed by a
    DSP56001 program (tools/fmdsp/fm_dsp.asm) instead of the 68k. Same sound,
    sample for sample (checked on the three music tracks in full, and in the
    game against the 68k emulation). The 68k only sends the register changes
    and reads the samples back: on a Falcon 030 the whole sound costs about
    15 ms per game step instead of 300+ ms with the FM music. The FM and PCM
    sound come out one game step (1/30 s) late.
  - Sound from a timer interrupt (sound_irq = 2, default: on with a
    68040/68060): the music keeps its tempo and has no holes even when the
    picture rate drops. Off with a 68030, which has no time to spare for it.
  - .mod music with DSPMOD 3.4 by bITmASTER of TCE (mod_dsp = 2). DSPMOD is
    written for the 68030: with a 68040/68060, and for modules with more than
    64 patterns, the modules are played by the Simplet DSP replay instead
    (as with mod_dsp = 1).
  - mod_dsp, the .mod replay (mod = 1, files in Music\):
      0 = mixed by the 68k;
      1 = played by the DSP, SoundTracker replay by Simplet / ABSTRACT
          (recommended, especially on CT60/CT63);
      2 = played by the DSP, DSPMOD 3.4 (bITmASTER of TCE), Falcon 030 only:
          with a 68040/68060, or for a module with more than 64 patterns,
          the game uses the Simplet replay (= 1). DSPMOD is not 68060-safe
          (it writes to the DSP without waiting for it and uses a 64-bit
          mulu.l, which the 68060 only emulates): it froze or slowed the
          game; and it hangs on modules with more than 64 patterns.
    While a .mod is played by the DSP, the FM sounds go back to the 68k.
  Controls
  - Joystick fixed on real CT60 hardware (enhanced ports read after a short
    settling delay; both DB9 ports read; IKBD set up through TOS).
  - Configurable joystick: joy_accel, joy_brake, joy_gear in outrun.ini
    (up, down, fire, b, c, none, combined with '+').
  - PADTEST.TOS: shows live what each joystick socket returns.

Layout of this delivery:
  cannonball_src/      - full source tree, no build artifacts, no ROM data.
  cannonball_release/  - CB030.TOS / CB060.TOS, roms/, Music/, outrun.ini.
